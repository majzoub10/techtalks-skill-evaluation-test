Language Skill Assessment – Rules & Scoring

How It Works

  • ▶Select a programming language
  • ▶Quiz starts immediately after confirmation
  • ▶Questions appear one at a time
  • ▶The quiz consists of 5 balanced questions
  • ▶3 Easy and 2 hard for a fair skill evaluation
  • ▶Final score shown instantly
  • ▶You can review your answers when you're done

Available Quizzes

  • 💻Python
  • ☕Java
  • 🟨JavaScript
  • 🐘PHP

Time Control

  • ⏱Easy questions: 20 seconds
  • ⏱Hard questions: 30 seconds
  • ❌Time over = wrong answer
  • ➡Automatically moves to the next question and a new count down will start
  • 🔒No returning to previous questions

Score Calculation

  • 🟢Easy questions weight: 40%
  • 🔴Hard questions weight: 60%
  • ⏳A question answered correctly after half of the given time will receive 50% of the available points
  • ❌Wrong or timed-out = 0 points
  • 📊Final score normalized to 100

Achievement Medals

  • 🥉Bronze: 40 – 59
  • 🥈Silver: 60 – 79
  • 🥇Gold: 80 – 100
  • 🏆Medal saved to your profile
  • 📜Used as skill verification

Attempt Policy

  • 🔁One attempt per language every 30 days
  • ⏳Cooldown starts after quiz submission
  • 🚫Same language locked during cooldown
  • ✅Other languages remain available
